**Snippet E1 – Draft & Core Principles**
During territory draft focus on *tempo* (acting before opponents can reinforce) and *logistics* (fewest borders per bonus). Concentrate armies in 2‑3 key territories; a spread of singletons slows you. In low‑card early rounds you cannot rely on a cash‑in safety net, so keep at least a 3:1 attacker‑to‑defender ratio on targets. Tempo > logistics > probability until Turn 3.

**Snippet E2 – Continental Bonus Math (0‑2 Cards)**
Australia and South America yield +2 for ≤ 2 borders—best ROI for small stacks. Africa (+3, 3 borders) is viable if opponents ignore it. Skip Europe/Asia unless already half‑owned. Break an enemy’s fragile bonus even at a –3 army trade: denying +2 early reinforcements swings momentum in your favour.

[phase:early;card_bucket:mid]
**Snippet E3 – Early Expansion Ratios (3‑4 Cards)**
With 3‑4 cards you’re two territories away from a set; attack neutrals aggressively to farm cards. Use 3 armies per 1‑army neutral (95 % win) or 5 per 2‑army. Always leave 2 behind each conquest. Prioritise taking a card over perfect odds—card acceleration is worth ~+3 armies per future turn once sets reach +8.

[phase:early;card_bucket:mid]
**Snippet E4 – Card Timing & Spoiler Tactics**
If the table set is ≤ +6, cash only to complete your continent in the same turn; otherwise hold. Hovering at 4 cards threatens a 5‑card spike next round and deters attacks against you. Park single armies on borders of rivals’ bonuses to force them into low‑odds cleans or delayed reinforcement.

[phase:early;card_bucket:high]
**Snippet E5 – High‑Card Risk (5+ Cards)**
Holding 5 cards early screams *bounty.* Assume opponents will spend ≤ 30 armies to wipe you. Stack at least 18 on your capital territory by Turn 3 or pre‑emptively cash, even for +4. A surprise early +4 often secures the last piece of your bonus and flips hunter into hunted.

[phase:early;card_bucket:high]
**Snippet E6 – Opportunistic Early Wipes**
Scan the board each turn: if a neighbour owns 5 cards and < 25 armies behind ≤ 3 borders, consider an elimination strike. Success nets their cards, letting you chain‑cash to +8. Calculate path cost: total defenders × 1.3 + 3 garrisons per captured territory. Launch only if ≥ 65 % success probability.

[phase:mid;card_bucket:low]
**Snippet M1 – Border Consolidation (0‑2 Cards)**
With few cards you must conserve armies. Trade interior territories for a single choke line (Siam, Middle East, Ukraine). Maintain a 1.2:1 defender ratio; bluff strength by visibly stacking 15+. Use non‑binding truces on static borders so you can redeploy to active fronts.

---

[phase:mid;card_bucket:low]
**Snippet M2 – Dice‑Odds Refresher**
3 v 2 rolls: 55 % attacker win; need ≥ 25 % numerical edge. 3 v 1: 66 % win but higher attrition. With no card cushion, avoid battles where expected net gain (ENG) < 0.5 territories. Cancel attacks after two straight defender wins; variance kills low‑card players.

---

[phase:mid;card_bucket:mid]
**Snippet M3 – Attack vs Fortify Decision (3‑4 Cards)**
Compute **ENG = P(win) × (territories + break_bonus) – P(loss) × armies_lost**. If ENG > 0 and attack reduces an enemy bonus, strike—even at 1:1 odds—because your soon‑to‑cash set offsets losses. Otherwise fortify toward a wipe target.

---

[phase:mid;card_bucket:mid]
**Snippet M4 – Optimal Cash‑In Window**
Turn in when set value is one step below the peak (e.g., +15 of +20 sequence). Use the armies to break a rival’s bonus *and* finish a card‑farming route. Dropping to 0 cards right before the peak keeps you un‑wipeable.

[phase:mid;card_bucket:high]
**Snippet M5 – Elimination Math (5+ Cards)**
A player with 5 cards is worth \~+20 armies next turn. Plan a kill path that costs ≤ 35 % of your stack while leaving ≥ 20 behind your borders. If elimination nets enough cards to cash, include the upcoming +20 in your ENG; chain‑cash wipes often decide mid‑games.

---

[phase:mid;card_bucket:high]
**Snippet M6 – Blitz Breaks**
Neutralise a continent in one go: sum defending armies along path plus garrisons; bring 1.4× that number. Expect ≥ 60 % success. After break, leave only blocking singles inside; funnel the rest to your main hammer stack to threaten a wipe next round.

[phase:late;card_bucket:low]
**Snippet L1 – Endgame Tempo (0‑2 Cards)**
Late game with no set imminent: avoid trading territory for cards—you will lose the arm‑race. Instead, fortify choke lines and bargain coalition strikes against the chip‑leaders. Keep your stack mobile (one multi‑continent border) to threaten retaliation.

---

[phase:late;card_bucket:low]
**Snippet L2 – Defensive Stacking**
Hold at least 1.3× the largest opponent stack on any border they can reach in one turn. If behind on card economy, let rivals clash first; pick off weakened continents the following turn to regain parity.

---

[phase:late;card_bucket:mid]
**Snippet L3 – Chain‑Cash Preparation (3‑4 Cards)**
Position your hammer stack within striking distance of two weak players. Cash to break first, wipe second, and immediately cash again. Reserve 10 extra armies for unforeseen defender high‑rolls; chain stalls lose games.

---

[phase:late;card_bucket:mid]
**Snippet L4 – Valuing Sets Late**
Each +25 set roughly equals a continent bonus for five turns. If table set will jump next turn, purposely take a card to reach 4 cards and threaten a +30. Force leaders to defend two fronts or risk a lethal wipe.

---

[phase:late;card_bucket:high]
**Snippet L5 – Final‑Stretch Eliminations (5+ Cards)**
Map out two‑hop kill chains: clearance cost ≤ 40 % of your stack, yield +25 set, fund next kill. Count exact defender armies; any mis‑calc lets the runner‑up clear you instead. If uncertain, cash pre‑emptively and fortify.

---

[phase:late;card_bucket:high]
**Snippet L6 – Race to 24‑Card Limit**
In 24‑card escalate variants, track opponents’ card counts like lives. The winner usually wipes two players in one turbo turn. Keep at least 15 spare armies after each wipe to survive a failed chain. Never end turn holding 5 cards and < 25 armies.
